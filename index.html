Anil Chandra Seth

Professor of Physics & Astronomy at the University of Utah

I work on understanding the formation of galaxies, and particularly the extreme objects we find at their centers, nuclear star clusters and massive black holes. I use dynamical and stellar population analysis applied to both imaging and spectroscopic data from telescopes including the James Webb Space Telescope, Hubble, and the Very Large Telescope. I am also a research mentor to a wonderful group of current and prior students, an advocate and educator about the importance of dark skies, and run an astronomy outreach internship at Bryce Canyon National Park.
