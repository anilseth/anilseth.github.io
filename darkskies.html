Dark Skies Advocacy and Outreach

Artificial light at night has serious impacts on human health, our ecology, and, of course, our ability to enjoy the night sky. I advocate for maintaining and improving the dark skies in Utah and our region. I helped start the world’s first Dark Sky Studies minor at the University of Utah and enjoy teaching courses in this program. I also serve on the board of Dark Sky Utah, a local chapter of Dark Sky International, which advocates for dark skies and designates dark sky places. I also founded an internship program for astronomy outreach at Bryce Canyon National Park.
